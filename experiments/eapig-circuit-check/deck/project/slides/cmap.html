Edge-level version of Prakash et al. 2024’s cross-model activation patching. Elicit: the parent’s own downstream weights turn the child’s circuit signals into the child’s answers (accuracy from 0 to 0.32–0.86), and nearly all of it rides on the edges the child shares with the parent’s op-form circuit; the child’s other circuit edges do no more than random edges of the same types. Teach: the same patch drives the format parent away from the child, so its downstream cannot use what the child learned. Edges into logits are left out because they hand the child’s answer straight to the unembedding (with them, recovery is 1.00 and 0.84 on the two routes). The first type-matched null shared 40–71% of its edges with the circuit and was replaced by the disjoint one shown here.
