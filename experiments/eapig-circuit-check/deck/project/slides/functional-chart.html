Share = how much of the child's own-circuit f you get when only a parent's circuit is live in the child. The thick blue line is near 1 at every size: the elicit parent's op-form circuit, dropped into the elicit child, does nearly all of the child's own circuit's work. The thin blue line with open rings is the elicit parent's NL circuit: it falls to about 0.3, so the reuse is specific to the op-form circuit. The teach child gets a quarter to two-fifths from its format parent (orange; 0.24–0.31 up to 2%, 0.41 at 5%), and from 0.5% up it gets more from the elicit parent's op circuit (gray control) than from its own parent. Random circuits give f of about zero. At 0.01–0.1% the child's own circuit recovers f of about 0 (at most 0.007), so the share divides by about zero and is not plotted (grey band).
