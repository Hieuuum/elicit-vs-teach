Share = how much of the child's own-circuit f you get when only a parent's circuit is live in the child. The thick blue line is near 1 at every size: the elicit parent's op-form circuit, dropped into the elicit child, does nearly all of the child's own circuit's work. The thin blue line with open rings is the elicit parent's NL circuit: it falls to about 0.3, so the reuse is specific to the op-form circuit. The teach child gets a quarter to a third from its format parent (orange), and from 0.5% up it gets more from the elicit parent's op circuit (gray control) than from its own parent. Random circuits give f of about zero.
