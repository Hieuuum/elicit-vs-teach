The elicit parent’s op-form circuit, dropped into the elicit child, does 86–98% of the work of the child’s own circuit. The teach child gets about a quarter to a third from its format parent’s circuit, and from 0.5% up gets more from the elicit parent’s op circuit (the control) than from its own parent: the teach child built something closer to the op-form addition circuit than to anything in its format parent.
