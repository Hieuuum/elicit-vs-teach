Functional reuse asks whether the parent's circuit can do the child's job inside the child. We run the child with only one edge set live; every other edge gets its activation from a corrupted prompt (a different problem), as in all circuit evals here. f measures how close the output stays to the full child, in KL. Panel 1 uses the child's own circuit, panel 2 uses the parent's circuit (the same number of top edges, chosen in the parent), and the share is their ratio. Share near 1 means the parent's circuit does nearly all the work of the child's own circuit. Numbers at 1%: elicit child own f 0.782, share 0.89 with the elicit parent's op-form circuit; teach child own f 0.516, share 0.29 with the format parent's circuit. The parent's f is recovered as share × own f, so it is rounded. Random circuits give f ≈ 0.
