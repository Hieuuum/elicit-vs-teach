Recovery = 1 − KL(child‖patched) ÷ KL(child‖parent). The parent runs with the child's activations on the child's circuit edges. Thick blue: the elicit child's circuit moves the parent almost all the way to the child (0.77 to 0.98; accuracy from 0 to 0.32–0.86). Thin blue with open rings: the child's circuit edges that are NOT in the parent's op-form circuit do no better than the null (0.03–0.18), so the effect rides on the shared edges (0.71–0.97, in the table on the next slide). Orange: the teach child's circuit pushes the format parent further from the child than it started (−0.83 to −1.19). The gray dotted null is the higher of the two routes' disjoint type-matched nulls at each size (elicit 0.03–0.24, teach 0.04–0.18).
